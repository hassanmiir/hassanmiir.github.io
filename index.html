🎯 About

I specialize in communication-efficient federated learning for resource-constrained environments, with a focus on system-level precision, reproducibility, and practical deployment at the edge. My research develops innovative methods and frameworks including GASPU, FedEdge, FedFor, and PA-FL to make distributed learning robust and production-ready.

My work sits at the intersection of distributed systems and machine learning: orchestrating resource-constrained devices, compressing and securing the learning signal, and evaluating end-to-end behavior with rigorous, reproducible pipelines. I'm passionate about building solutions that translate from paper to production, pushing evaluation beyond accuracy into reliability, privacy, safety, and sustainability.
